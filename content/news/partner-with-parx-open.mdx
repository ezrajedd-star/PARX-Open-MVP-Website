PARX Open is now inviting brands to partner with the series for its first season.

## Packages per leg

Sponsorship is sold per leg, so brands can focus on the cities that matter to them:

- **Title** — naming rights for the leg and top placement everywhere
- **Gold** — premium placement on cars, signage and content
- **Silver** and **Bronze** — strong, affordable venue and online presence
- **Local Partner** — in-kind and X-deals for hotels, catering and venues

Brands that want to own the whole season can take a **season bundle** covering all five legs.

## Sponsor a driver or a team

Brands can also back individual drivers or whole teams. These deals are negotiated directly with the drivers and team principals — see the [Teams & Drivers](/teams) page.
