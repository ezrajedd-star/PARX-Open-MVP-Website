The opening PARX Open grid brings together six teams and a group of independent drivers. Here is a quick introduction — full driver profiles live on the [Teams & Drivers](/teams) page.

## The teams

- **Team Ormoc** — led by team principal Toto Locsin, with the biggest roster on the grid.
- **Team Cebu** — led by Jong King.
- **Cebu Racing Group** — led by Jess Garcia.
- **Gagoo Racing Team** — led by Khalil Alquilos.
- **R10 RallyX** — led by Jam Malig.
- **GGC/Garcia Racing Team** — led by Paulo Garcia.

## The independents

Not every driver races under a team banner. George, Ivan and Vip line up as free agents, scoring in the drivers' championship on their own.
