**PARX Open — the Pilipinas Archipelago RallyCross Open — is a new championship built for the dirt roads, fans and towns of the Visayas and Mindanao.**

The series runs over five legs across two island groups, with a champion crowned at the final round. Each leg is a full race weekend: Parc Exposé and a welcome party on Friday, eight timed stages across Saturday and Sunday, and an awarding ceremony on Sunday evening.

## Five legs, two island groups

The season visits:

- **Round 1 — Ormoc**, Leyte
- **Round 2 — Tabogon**, Cebu
- **Round 3 — Iloilo**, Iloilo
- **Round 4 — Bacolod**, Negros Occidental
- **Round 5 — Bukidnon**, Northern Mindanao

Dates for every leg will be announced on this website and on our Facebook page.

## A championship, not a club meet

Every stage is timed, every result counts and the standings update after every leg. Points are awarded to the top ten finishers, and both drivers' and teams' championships are decided over the full season.
